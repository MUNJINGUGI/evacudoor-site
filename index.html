When the network fails, the exit still shows.

Evacudoor is an offline fire evacuation guidance system for commercial buildings in Kenya. It keeps pointing people to the safest exit when WiFi, mobile networks and alarms have gone down.

Get in touch

In a fire, the systems meant to help are often the first to fail.

When fire breaks out in a commercial building, people often don't know which exit is safest. Most buildings in Kenya still rely on static signage and human memory in the middle of a crisis.

The tools we expect to help depend on infrastructure that fire and smoke can take out quickly.

Guidance that keeps working when the building doesn't.

Evacudoor is a network of small, smart devices installed throughout a building. They sense conditions and guide people to the safest exit in real time.

Works offline

No internet or cellular connection is needed, so guidance keeps running when infrastructure doesn't.

Reacts to conditions

Guidance updates as conditions change, so people are not sent toward danger.

Made for Kenyan buildings

Designed in Nairobi for the way commercial buildings here are built and used.

How it works

  1. Sense

    Small devices placed through the building detect heat and smoke.

  2. Decide

    The system works out which exits are still safe, with no internet or cellular signal.

  3. Guide

    People are shown the safest way out, updating as conditions change.
